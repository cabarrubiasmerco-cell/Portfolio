OPINION

We humans need to protect our oceans and seas because they are vital to life on Earth. Oceans help regulate our climate, produce oxygen, and provide food and jobs for millions of people. They are home to a huge variety of animals and plants, many of which we still don’t fully understand. Oceans also help fight climate change by absorbing carbon dioxide and heat. If we damage our oceans through pollution, overfishing, or other harmful actions, it can hurt marine life, human health, and the global climate. So, taking care of our oceans is crucial for keeping our planet and ourselves healthy.
