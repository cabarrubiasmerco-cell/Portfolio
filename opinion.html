Our position

Protect the ocean.
Choose cooperation.

Two connected responsibilities shape our position: caring for marine ecosystems and addressing disputes with fairness, restraint, and respect.

01
Ocean stewardship

Our seas are life support systems.

Oceans regulate climate, produce oxygen, absorb carbon, provide food, and sustain countless species. Pollution, overfishing, and habitat destruction place marine life and human communities at risk. Protecting the ocean is therefore an environmental, economic, public-health, and ethical responsibility.

“A healthy ocean is essential to a healthy planet and a secure future.”
02
Regional cooperation

Greed and pride must not guide policy.

Territorial disputes such as those surrounding Scarborough Shoal should be approached through peaceful dialogue and international law. Excessive pride can prevent compromise, while greed can encourage the exploitation of both resources and vulnerable communities.

“Shared waters require shared responsibility.”
